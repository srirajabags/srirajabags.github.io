**Sri Raja Bags makes printed and plain plastic covers at our unit in Dhone and supplies shops across Andhra Pradesh, Telangana and Karnataka.** We make D-cut covers in HM and LD plastic, U-cut covers, and bio-degradable U-cut covers, printed with your shop name in one or two colours or left plain. Rates are per kg, and the more you order, the lower the rate.

## Covers we make

{{< covers-grid >}}

- **HM D-cut covers** are the crisp, rustling covers with a punched handle, used by clothing, footwear and general shops. They are the most economical printed cover we make.
- **LD D-cut covers** are softer and clearer than HM, for showrooms that want the cover to feel smoother in the hand.
- **U-cut covers** are the vest-shaped covers with a gusset, so they open wide and stand on the counter. Hotels, bakeries, kirana stores and supermarkets use them for quick billing.
- **Bio-degradable U-cut covers** are made in the same shape and sizes as the plastic U-cut cover.

The common sizes are **10×14, 12×18, 14×20 and 16×20 inches**. Each cover's page lists every size we make, and other sizes can be made to order.

## Common questions

**Do you print on plastic covers?**
Yes. We print your shop name, address and phone number in one or two colours, in Telugu, English or both. Plain covers without printing are also available.

**What is the difference between HM and LD covers?**
HM covers are crisper and more economical. LD covers are softer and clearer. Both come with a punched D-cut handle. See [LD vs HM covers](/blog/2026/10/29/ld-vs-hm-covers/) for how to choose.

**Which sizes do you make?**
10×14, 12×18, 14×20 and 16×20 inches are the most common. Each cover's page lists every size, and other sizes can be made to order.
