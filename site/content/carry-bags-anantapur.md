## Common questions

**How long does delivery take?**
About 15–20 days for printing after you approve the design proof, then 1–2 days by transport to your town.

**Is freight included in the rate?**
No. Rates are ex-factory; we quote the transport charge for your town with your order.

**What is the minimum order?**
10 kg for D-cut and handle bags, 30 kg for U-cut bags, and 200 bags for stitched bags.
