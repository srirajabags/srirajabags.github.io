## Which bag for which shop

- **Saree and silk showrooms:** a stitched bag with a side patty. A folded saree sits flat instead of creasing, and the bag stands on the counter. The side patty comes in widths from 4 to 12 inch. For wedding purchases, a [BOPP laminated bag](/products/digital-bopp/) with a full colour print is reused for months.
- **Readymade, family and men's wear shops:** an 80 GSM D-cut bag for most bills, with HM covers for small purchases.
- **Kids wear, innerwear and fancy shops:** [HM covers](/products/hm-plastic-dcut/), the lowest cost per bill.
- **Boutiques and designer wear:** [LD covers](/products/ld-plastic-dcut/), which feel softer and look glossier, or a [handle bag](/products/handle-single-color/). See [LD vs HM covers](/blog/2026/10/29/ld-vs-hm-covers/) for the difference.
- **Tailors and matching centres:** D-cut bags or HM covers to send stitched clothes home.

## Common questions

**Can I order bags and covers together?**
Yes. The rate is decided by the value of the whole order, so different bags, covers and sizes add up.

**Which is better for a clothing shop, a non woven bag or a plastic cover?**
A cover is the cheapest way to hand goods over the counter. A non woven bag costs more, but customers reuse it for weeks, so your shop name travels further. Most clothing shops order both.

**Do you print in Telugu?**
Yes. We print your shop name, address and phone number in Telugu, Kannada, English or a mix, in one or two colours. Full colour prints are available on digital and BOPP laminated bags.

**What GSM should a clothing shop choose?**
80 GSM for D-cut and handle bags, 110 GSM for stitched bags with a side patty. See [non woven bag GSM explained](/blog/2026/10/16/non-woven-bag-gsm/).

**Where do you deliver?**
We make everything at Dhone, in Nandyal district of Andhra Pradesh, and send it by transport to shops across Andhra Pradesh, Telangana and Karnataka. See the [areas we supply](/areas-we-supply/).
