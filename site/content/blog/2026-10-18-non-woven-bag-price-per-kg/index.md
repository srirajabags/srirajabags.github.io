## What decides the rate per kg

**1. The bag type.** A D-cut bag has its handle punched into the bag, so it is the cheapest to make. A sewn loop handle costs more. A U-cut costs a little more per kg than a D-cut, but its 60 GSM fabric is lighter, so each kilo holds more bags; per bag it is the cheapest we make. Our [guide to bag types](/blog/2026/10/17/types-of-non-woven-bags/) explains which suits which shop.

**2. The value of your whole order.** Each bag has three rates: for orders above ₹40,000, from ₹20,000 to ₹40,000, and below ₹20,000. Everything in one order counts towards the total, so different sizes and bag types add up.

**3. The print.** One or two colours of offset print are included in the rate. A design that covers the whole bag (a full background) or a multi-colour design adds ₹20 per kg. The small 9 × 11 inch D-cut is printed on one side; printing both sides adds ₹30 per kg.

## From price per kg to price per bag

The number of bags in a kilo depends on the size and the GSM. For a rough count, work out the weight of the fabric in one bag:

**grams per bag ≈ width × height (in inches) × 2 sides × 0.000645 × GSM**

A 12 × 18 inch bag in 80 GSM: 12 × 18 × 2 × 0.000645 × 80 ≈ 22 g, so about 45 bags in a kilo. Here is what that means per bag for 80 GSM D-cut bags:

| Size | Bags per kg (approx.) | Over ₹40k | ₹20k–40k | Under ₹20k |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 × 14 in | ~69 | ~₹3.55 | ~₹3.70 | ~₹3.85 |
| 12 × 18 in | ~45 | ~₹5.45 | ~₹5.70 | ~₹5.95 |
| 16 × 20 in | ~30 | ~₹8.10 | ~₹8.40 | ~₹8.80 |

These are estimates from the fabric alone. The punched handle removes a little fabric and the ink adds a little, so ask us for the pieces per kg in your size before you order.

## How to compare two quotes per kg

A lower rate per kg is not always a lower price. Before you compare, check:

1. **The GSM.** At the same rate per kg, a 70 GSM bag gives more bags per kilo than an 80 GSM bag, but each bag is weaker. Ask for the GSM in writing, and [check it yourself](/blog/2026/10/16/non-woven-bag-gsm/) with a 10 × 10 cm square and a kitchen scale.
2. **Printed or plain.** Some rates are for plain bags. Ours include one or two colours of print.
3. **What is extra.** Block charges, GST and freight: is the quote ex-factory or delivered to your town?
4. **Payment terms.** Our rates are for 50% advance and the balance at billing.
5. **The cost per bag.** Ask both suppliers how many bags of your size come in a kilo, and divide. That is the number that matters at your counter.
