We have been manufacturing non-woven carry bags and plastic covers from our unit in Dorapalli, Dhone since 2011. Over that time, the questions our customers ask have stayed remarkably consistent: which bag suits my shop, how much weight will it hold, and what is actually the difference between all these bag types?

This blog is where we will answer those questions properly.

## What to expect

We will write about the practical side of packaging — the differences between D-cut and W-cut bags, when a loop-handle stitched bag is worth the extra cost, how printing works and what artwork we need from you, and how to think about durability versus price for your particular kind of shop.

We will also share what is changing in the industry. Regulations around single-use plastics keep evolving, materials keep improving, and it is not always obvious what any of it means for a small retailer placing an order of a few thousand bags.

## Questions are welcome

If there is something you want explained, write to us and we will cover it here. The most useful posts usually start as a customer question.
