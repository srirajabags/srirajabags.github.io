## Design ideas that work at Sankranti

{{< photo src="bopp-saree-showroom.jpg" alt="A BOPP laminated stitched bag for a saree showroom, with a full colour photo of a model in a green silk saree" caption="A full colour BOPP bag for a saree showroom. Photos like this need digital printing." >}}

- **Your name, big and clear.** Customers carry your bag through the busiest market days of the year. A bold shop name is seen across the street; the phone number and address can be smaller.
- **Bright fabric with one strong ink colour.** On a D-cut bag, a red or green fabric with your name printed in one colour stands out in a crowd, at the price of an ordinary printed bag.
- **Festive art needs full colour.** Kites, rangoli patterns, a family shopping scene or a model in a silk saree need digital printing. Choose a digital D-cut, a digital stitched bag or a BOPP bag.
- **A greeting, kept small.** "సంక్రాంతి శుభాకాంక్షలు" in a corner says it is the festival without taking over the bag.

{{< tip "Smart tip" >}}
Leave dates, "2027" and offer lines off the design. Then any bags left after the festival are still good to hand out in February, and nothing goes to waste.
{{< /tip >}}

## How many bags to order

Look at how many bags you used last Sankranti and in the weeks before it, from your stock book or your last order, and order a little more if your sales have grown. If this is your first printed bag, tell us roughly how many customers you serve on a busy day and we will suggest a quantity. Printed D-cut and handle bags are sold by the kilo, so we will also tell you about how many bags a kilo gives in your size.

Ordering a little more can cost less: the value of your whole order decides the rate per kg, and [our price-per-kg guide](/blog/2026/10/18/non-woven-bag-price-per-kg/) shows how.

## How to order

1. **Send your design on WhatsApp:** a logo, a visiting card, a photo of your shop board or of the bag you use now. No designer needed.
2. **Get a rate and a design proof,** the same day or the next.
3. **Approve the proof and pay the 50% advance.** Printing takes about 15 to 20 days.
4. **Delivery by transport,** 1 to 4 days to your town. The balance is paid at billing.

**When should I order Sankranti bags?**
Approve your design by 10 December 2026 for bags in your shop by about 3 January 2027. After 15 December, ask us for a delivery date first.

**When is Sankranti in 2027?**
Bhogi is on Thursday, 14 January 2027, with Sankranti and Kanuma on the two days after it in most Telugu calendars.

10 kg for printed D-cut and two-colour handle bags, 50 kg per size for digital D-cut bags, and 1,000 bags for digital stitched bags.

**Can you print my Sankranti greeting in Telugu?**
Yes. We print in Telugu, English or both, and you approve a proof before anything is printed.
