## Step 1: Choose how your bag is printed

There are three ways to print a shop name, and the right one depends on your design and budget.

| | Offset print (1–3 colours) | Full-colour digital | BOPP laminated |
|---|---|---|---|
| Looks like | Clean solid colours | Photographs, gradients, any design | Digital print under a glossy film |
| Best for | Name, logo, address, phone | Photos of models, jewellery, products | Premium showrooms, wedding season |
| Bag types | D-cut, handle, stitched | Stitched with side panels | Stitched with side panels |
| Priced | Per kg (D-cut, handle) or per bag (stitched) | Per bag | Per bag |
| Minimum order | 10 kg / 200 bags | 1,000 bags | 1,000 bags |

If your design is your shop name, logo and phone number, offset print is the economical choice. If it is a photograph, choose digital. For more on the laminated option, see our guide to [BOPP bags](/blog/2026/10/13/what-are-bopp-bags/).

## Step 3: Block charges, and how to pay them only once

For offset printing, each colour in your design needs a printing block (plate), which is a one-time charge on top of the bag price:

| Design | One-time use | Kept for a year |
|---|---|---|
| Single colour | ₹150 | ₹300 |
| Double colour | ₹400 | ₹800 |
| Three colour | ₹600 | — |
| Multi-colour or full-colour digital | ₹800 | ₹1,600 |
| BOPP laminated | ₹800 | — |

The charge covers the same design printed on both sides. A different design on each side costs extra — ask us for a quote. Three ways to save:

- **Keep your block for a year** if you reorder often — you pay once and every reorder is block-free. A single-colour design can also be made permanent for ₹500, as long as the size and design do not change.
- **Share one block across sizes.** Sizes of similar width with the same print colour can use the same block: 10 and 12 inch, 12 and 14 inch, or 14 and 16 inch.
- **Keep the design year-free.** Leave dates and "Diwali offer" lines off the bag, so leftover stock is still usable next season.

## Step 4: Pick colours that print well

On white and ivory bags almost any ink works — red, blue, black, green, magenta, violet and brown — and two-colour combinations such as red with green, red with black, or red with blue look sharp.

On coloured fabric, keep to dark ink. Green, red and pink bags are printed in black, and light yellow bags in black or red. Two-colour printing is not offered on coloured fabric.

## Common questions

**Can you print in Telugu?**
Yes. Most of the bags we print carry Telugu, English or both. Send the text exactly as you want it and check it on the proof.

**Do I pay the block charge every time I reorder?**
Only if you choose one-time use. Keep the block for a year (₹300 for single colour) and reorders within that year have no block charge.

**Can I order different sizes with one design?**
Yes. If the sizes are close in width and the print colour is the same, they can often share one block — 10 and 12 inch, for example.

**Is there a printing charge on top of the bag price?**
The bag rate includes printing. The only extra is the one-time block charge, plus GST and freight.

**Can I get a small trial order?**
The minimums are 10 kg for D-cut and handle bags and 200 for stitched bags. Ask on WhatsApp if you need fewer.
