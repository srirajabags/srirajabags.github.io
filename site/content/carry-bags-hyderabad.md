**Sri Raja Bags makes printed non woven carry bags at Dhone, on the Hyderabad–Bengaluru highway (NH 44), and sends them to shops in Hyderabad and Secunderabad by transport in 1–2 days.** In the last year, {{< area-count >}} shops in the city ordered bags from us, and more than a third of them came back for a second order. Most are fashion and garment showrooms, saree shops, designers and tailors; mobile shops and traders order too.

## What Hyderabad shops order

- **Printed D-cut bags in one colour** are what Hyderabad shops order most: more than half of the bags we sent to the city last year. Shop name and phone number in Telugu, English or both, on white or coloured fabric. [See D-cut rates →](/products/dcut-single-color/)
- **Two colour D-cut bags** when the logo needs a second colour. [See two colour rates →](/products/dcut-double-color/)
- **Stitched bags with a side patty**, single or full colour, for showrooms that want a bag customers reuse. [See stitched bags →](/products/stitching-single-color/)
- **Plain bags** without printing, for shops that need bags quickly.

The sizes Hyderabad shops pick most are 10×14 and 12×18 inches for small items, and 14×20, 16×18 and 16×20 inches for clothes and sarees. Not sure about the size or the fabric weight? See our [guide to bag types](/blog/2026/10/17/types-of-non-woven-bags/) and our [GSM guide](/blog/2026/10/16/non-woven-bag-gsm/).

## How ordering works from Hyderabad

1. **Send your design on WhatsApp, or call us.** A logo, a visiting card, a photo of your shop board or of the bag you use now is enough, with the size and quantity.
2. **Get a rate and a design proof** the same day or the next day.
3. **Approve the proof and pay the 50% advance.** Printing takes about 15–20 days.
4. **Delivery by transport, 1–2 days** after the bags are ready. Freight is quoted for your area; the balance is paid at billing.

Everything from the design to the payment happens on WhatsApp and the phone.

## Common questions

**How long does delivery to Hyderabad take?**
About 15–20 days for printing after you approve the design proof, then 1–2 days by transport to Hyderabad or Secunderabad.

**What is the minimum order?**
10 kg for D-cut and handle bags (in 80 GSM, about 300 bags of 16×20 inches or 690 of 10×14 inches), 30 kg for U-cut bags, and 200 bags for stitched bags.

**Can you print in Telugu?**
Yes. We print your shop name and details in Telugu, English or both, from your logo, visiting card or a photo of your shop board.

**Do you keep my printing block for the next order?**
Yes. The block is made once and kept at the factory for your next order.
