## When to choose a digital D-cut bag

An offset printed D-cut bag prints one or two flat colours. A digital D-cut bag prints the whole design in full colour: photographs of models and sarees, colour gradients and fine detail in Telugu or English lettering. It is the bag showrooms choose when the bag itself is an advertisement.

It comes in two sizes, **13×19 and 16×19 inches**, and three finishes:

- **Normal:** printed fabric. The lowest rate of the three.
- **Outside lamination:** a thin film over the print that protects it from rubbing and moisture, for ₹10 more per kg.
- **BOPP:** a glossy printed film laminated onto the fabric, for ₹25 more per kg than normal. The most premium look, used by saree and silk showrooms. See [what BOPP bags are](/blog/2026/10/13/what-are-bopp-bags/).

The minimum is **50 kg per size**, higher than the 10 kg of a [single colour D-cut bag](/products/dcut-single-color/). If you need fewer bags, or a size other than these two, a one or two colour D-cut bag is the better choice. For heavier goods, the same full colour print comes on a [digital handle bag](/products/digital-handle/) and a [BOPP handle bag](/products/digital-bopp-handle/).
