## About BOPP handle bags

A flat bag with loop handles and no side panel, printed in full colour under a glossy BOPP (Biaxially Oriented Polypropylene) film. It suits shops that want the BOPP finish without the bulk of a gusseted bag. If you need a side panel for boxed goods, see the [BOPP laminated bag with side panel](/products/digital-bopp/).
