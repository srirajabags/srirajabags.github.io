## When to choose a two colour handle bag

A two colour handle bag is our [single colour handle bag](/products/handle-single-color/) with a second ink. Most shops use the second colour to set the shop name apart from the address and phone strip, or to print a logo in its own brand colour. The loop handle is sewn on and can be matched to that colour, so the bag carries your brand from the handle down.

**What stays the same as the single colour bag:** the same 80 GSM fabric, the same seven sizes from 12×16 to 16×20 inches, the same 10 kg minimum and the same rate per kg, shown in the price list above. **What changes:** the second colour needs its own printing block, a one-time charge quoted with your order, and the fabric comes in white or ivory.

Choose it over a [two colour D-cut bag](/products/dcut-double-color/) when the goods are heavy: footwear, jewellery boxes, sarees and stacks of garments. A sewn handle spreads the weight, where a punched D-cut handle can tear. For a full colour design with photographs, see the [digital handle bag](/products/digital-handle/) instead. A full background colour or more than two colours adds ₹20/kg.
