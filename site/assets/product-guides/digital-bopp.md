## About BOPP laminated bags

BOPP (Biaxially Oriented Polypropylene) is a thin glossy film laminated over the printed fabric. It keeps the print from rubbing off, lets the bag be wiped clean and gives it a photo-like shine, which is why saree, jewellery and textile showrooms choose it for bags customers keep for months. Compared with a [normal digital bag](/products/digital-multi-color/) of the same size it costs about ₹1.50–₹2 more per bag.

For the full comparison, the other BOPP types (flat handle bag, D-cut) and how to choose, see our guide: [What are BOPP bags?](/blog/2026/10/13/what-are-bopp-bags/)
