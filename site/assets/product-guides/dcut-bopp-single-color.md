## About BOPP D-cut bags

A D-cut bag with a punched handle and a one colour print covered by a glossy BOPP (Biaxially Oriented Polypropylene) film. The film protects the print and gives a shine at a lower cost per bag than a full colour BOPP bag. For a full colour design, see the [digital D-cut bag in BOPP finish](/products/digital-dcut/).

More on BOPP finishes, types and prices: [What are BOPP bags?](/blog/2026/10/13/what-are-bopp-bags/)
