## When to choose a non woven U-cut bag

The U-cut is the vest-shaped bag, with two straps and a gusset, so it opens wide and stands on the counter while you fill it. It is made for quick billing: restaurant parcels, bakery counters, kirana and supermarket checkouts, wine shops and medical shops.

Our U-cut bags are **60 GSM**, the lightest fabric allowed for non woven carry bags. Light fabric means more bags in every kilo, which is why the U-cut costs the least per bag of anything we make. They come in white, printed with your shop name, in four sizes: **10×14, 13×16, 16×20 and 20×20 inches**. Pick the size by what goes in it, from small items like medicines at 10×14 up to large grocery orders at 20×20.

The minimum is **30 kg**; the price list above has the rate for each size. A 60 GSM bag is used a few times, not reused for a season. If you want customers to keep your bag for months, choose an 80 GSM [D-cut](/products/dcut-single-color/) or [handle bag](/products/handle-single-color/) instead.
